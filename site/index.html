Residue-field extensions of O'Brien's examples for Arvind–Cameron–Ma–Maslova's commuting-graph question

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

For Arvind–Cameron–Ma–Maslova's Question 4, second sentence, in Section 9, p. 40 of arXiv:2305.07301v5 (27 July 2025), we extend the examples attributed there to Eamonn O'Brien to non-isoclinic groups of order q^6 with isomorphic full commuting graphs for every prime power q = p^f, including characteristic two, using residue-field coordinate charts for Heisenberg groups over two rings of order q^2. Their derived groups are (C_(p^2))^f and (C_p)^(2f). The common graph is chordal and perfect but neither a cograph, a split graph nor a threshold graph; its clique structure and degree distribution are explicit. The case f = 1 answers the posed all-prime question, and abelian factors give examples of every order p^n with n >= 6.
